Selected work

From a small start, making waves.

Bloomora

E-commerce platform · Floral business

01

Bloomora was a year-long journey, from ideation to turnover. It began in 2025 when our team went door-to-door around the University Belt in search of a client. That search eventually brought us to Dangwa, where we met Esting’s, the flower shop that became our capstone partner. What followed was a year of consistent communication, feedback, and refinement with our client, mentor, and panelists across three defenses.

I served as the team’s lead documenter and lead quality assurance member. As lead documenter, I continually reviewed and refined our capstone paper to ensure it met academic standards and clearly explained our system, processes, and decisions to the panelists evaluating our work.

Quality assurance was especially demanding because system development was concentrated into just one to two months. I worked closely with our developers to thoroughly test Bloomora’s modules, identify issues, and verify fixes. Nightly testing became part of my routine, giving our developers the timely feedback they needed to keep moving. This required careful attention to detail and quick turnaround times as changes were implemented.

I also maintained our Scrum task list, adjusting priorities as development progressed. Each day brought new findings and challenges, requiring us to add tasks, reprioritize issues, or remove items that were no longer relevant.

The experience was exhausting, but seeing our team pass the final defense with minor revisions made the effort worthwhile. More recently, I took the lead in presenting our study to an external panel of judges and sharing our work with fellow students. Standing before that audience filled me with pride as I spoke about the project our team had spent a year bringing to life.

Bloomora taught me how much clear documentation, consistent testing, and close collaboration contribute to delivering a system. It also strengthened my ability to communicate findings, adapt to changing priorities, and support a team under tight deadlines.
